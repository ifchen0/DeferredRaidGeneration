[b]Warning:[/b] this mod was written with AI coding tools (Claude Code). It changes when and how raid and quest pawns are created: large groups are generated over several seconds, then the vanilla raid or quest code runs a second time with those pawns. Nothing is stored in your save. Mods that change a raid as it starts (for example its faction or size) may lose that effect on deferred raids; mods that act after the raid arrives are not affected. Handled so far: Breaking News' mechanoid advance. Tested in one late-game colony only. If raids, beggar/refugee quests or Dynamic Diplomacy battles behave oddly, disable this mod before reporting the issue to other mod authors.

Removes most of the multi-second freeze when a large raid fires, and the pause when large beggar or refugee groups show up.

[h2]Seconds of freeze before the raid letter[/h2]Normally every raider is generated in the same frame the raid fires, so a raid of 100+ pawns freezes the game for several seconds before the letter shows up. In a late-game colony each pawn costs 15-35 ms to generate, and the game also repeats a scan of every container on every map ([i]MapPawns.AllPawnsUnspawned[/i]) dozens of times per pawn.

[b]Fix:[/b] When a raid of 5 or more pawns fires, its faction, strategy, arrival mode and pawn kinds are decided by the vanilla code as usual. The pawns are then generated one at a time, a quarter of a second apart, while the game keeps running. Once all of them exist, the vanilla raid code runs again and receives these pawns instead of generating new ones, so arrival, letter, lords and loot are unchanged. The raid shows up later than it otherwise would: about 0.25 s of real time per pawn, at most about 20 s by default (adjustable in the mod settings, 5-60 s).

[h2]Beggar and refugee groups[/h2]The beggar quest and the refugee quest bring a group sized to your colony (a quarter to three quarters of your colonists, plus their generated relatives), all generated in the frame the quest appears. Refugees also all join your faction and spawn in the frame you accept.

[b]Fix:[/b] Groups of 5 or more are generated the same way as raids: group size, children and their temporary faction are decided first, the pawns are generated over a few seconds, then the vanilla quest is created with them. After you accept, the first refugee joins at once and the others join and walk in one per frame.

[h2]Also[/h2][list]
[*]For all pawn generation, the container scan is done once per map for each pawn or pawn group instead of dozens of times.
[*]With Humanoid Alien Races, starting apparel no longer copies and filters the full apparel list for every pawn: the race-filtered list is built once per race (same HAR rule, same result).
[*]Pawn graphics are built the first time a pawn is drawn, 2-5 ms each with HAR. Deferred pawns get them during generation; other pawns on the map are prepared in the background at about 1 ms per frame.
[*]Arriving groups start looking for their first job spread over half a second, and the "remove died thoughts" check on spawn only looks at pawns that remember one of them, instead of every pawn in the world.
[*]Raider steal checks compute each nearby item's market value once per check instead of once per raider (corpses included). Same idea as [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3781897112]Raider Approach Lag Fix[/url] by OldManYoung, extended to the stealing phase; the two work together.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Applies to enemy and friendly raids and beggar/refugee quests with at least 5 pawns. Smaller groups, trader caravans, quest raids, and raid types that create their own threats (for example mechanoid sieges) run immediately as in vanilla.
[*]With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3220299022]Dynamic Diplomacy - Continued[/url], the armies of a full-simulation conquest arena are generated the same way. The arena map itself is still generated in one go when the battle starts.
[*]Each generation step still costs one pawn's generation time (usually 15-30 ms, occasionally over 100 ms), so you may notice a light stutter while a large group is being prepared. While you move or zoom the camera, steps wait until it has been still for 0.4 s (at most 10 s per group).
[*]An autosave that comes due while pawns are being generated waits until they are done (at most a minute). A manual save in that window finishes them first, so nothing is lost.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]Every feature can be switched off in the mod settings (takes effect after a restart); a feature that is off does not patch the game at all.
[/list]

[h2]Test report[/h2]One late-game colony with a large mod list (Humanoid Alien Races, Miho, Dynamic Diplomacy; 167 active mods in total), about 1,100-1,500 world pawns. Numbers are real-time milliseconds from development-mode logs on RimWorld 1.7 (2026-10-14), with the maximum preparation time set to 60 s. Lines marked (1.6) were measured on 1.6 and have not been re-run yet. Also active: Performance Optimizer, Performance - Slower Pawn Tick Rate, Performance Esmolas.

[h3]Raids[/h3][list]
[*]Vanilla: an enemy raid of 176 pawns froze for 2.6 s. (1.6) Dynamic Diplomacy arenas of 234-258 pawns froze for 12.3 s, 31.6 s with several old arena maps loaded.
[*]Enemy raids of 169-178 pawns: arrival pause 39-55 ms; each group was generated over 47-57 s.
[*]Generation steps: 11-19 ms on average; worst single step 98 ms.
[*](1.6) Dynamic Diplomacy arenas of 255-324 pawns: armies generated over 34-73 s; the battle start (map generation) still paused 1.7-7 s.
[*](1.6) Saving or loading while groups were pending: saved groups arrived intact, nothing carried over into a loaded game; a due autosave waited 19.1 s, then saved.
[*]Pawn graphics: every raider's graphics were ready on arrival; the first frame on screen still took 160-200 ms, spent on the game's texture atlas (about 1.3-1.5 ms per pawn, not covered by the warmup).
[*]Steal checks (map full of corpses and gear from earlier raids): 3.7-5.6 ms per check without the cache, 1.3-1.9 ms with it.
[/list]
[h3]Beggar and refugee quests[/h3][list]
[*](1.6) Vanilla: groups of 12-30 froze for 0.74-1.07 s when the quest appeared; accepting 43 refugees froze for 0.59 s.
[*]With this mod: groups of 47 / 53 generated over 18 s (steps 13 ms on average), quest appeared with a 0.27 / 0.16 s pause; accepting 53 refugees took 35 ms, all walked in over 1.6 s.
