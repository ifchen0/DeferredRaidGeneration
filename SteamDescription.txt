[b]Warning:[/b] this mod was written with AI coding tools (Claude Code). It changes when and how raid and quest pawns are created: large groups are generated over several seconds, then the vanilla raid or quest code runs a second time with those pawns. On maps with 100 or more pawns it also runs walking, waiting and downed pawns at a reduced tick rate (can be turned off in the mod settings). Nothing is stored in your save. Mods that change a raid as it starts (for example its faction or size) may lose that effect on deferred raids; mods that act after the raid arrives are not affected. Handled so far: Breaking News' mechanoid advance. Tested in one late-game colony only. If raids, beggar/refugee quests or Dynamic Diplomacy battles behave oddly, disable this mod before reporting the issue to other mod authors.

Removes most of the multi-second freeze when a large raid fires, and the pause when large beggar or refugee groups show up. On crowded maps it also lightens the per-tick work of pawns that are only walking, waiting or lying downed.

[h2]Seconds of freeze before the raid letter[/h2]Normally every raider is generated in the same frame the raid fires, so a raid of 100+ pawns freezes the game for several seconds before the letter shows up. In a late-game colony each pawn costs 15-35 ms to generate, and the game also repeats a scan of every container on every map ([i]MapPawns.AllPawnsUnspawned[/i]) dozens of times per pawn.

[b]Fix:[/b] When a raid of 5 or more pawns fires, its faction, strategy, arrival mode and pawn kinds are decided by the vanilla code as usual. The pawns are then generated one at a time, a quarter of a second apart, while the game keeps running. Once all of them exist, the vanilla raid code runs again and receives these pawns instead of generating new ones, so arrival, letter, lords and loot are unchanged. The raid shows up later than it otherwise would: about 0.25 s of real time per pawn, at most about 20 s.

[h2]Beggar and refugee groups[/h2]The beggar quest and the refugee quest bring a group sized to your colony (a quarter to three quarters of your colonists, plus their generated relatives), all generated in the frame the quest appears. Refugees also all join your faction and spawn in the frame you accept.

[b]Fix:[/b] Groups of 5 or more are generated the same way as raids: group size, children and their temporary faction are decided first, the pawns are generated over a few seconds, then the vanilla quest is created with them. After you accept, the first refugee joins at once and the others join and walk in one per frame.

[h2]Crowded maps[/h2]On a map with many pawns (a large raid, a Dynamic Diplomacy arena) most of them are only walking, waiting or lying downed, yet each runs its full update every tick.

[b]Fix:[/b] On maps with 100 or more pawns, such pawns run their full update only every 4th tick. In between, movement, ability cooldowns, sounds and visual effects still update, so they move and look as before. Pawns doing any other job keep the normal rate (work progress counts per tick), as do drafted colonists and pawns that attacked or were hurt in the last game hour. The rate and the pawn threshold can be changed, or the feature turned off, in the mod settings.

[h2]Also[/h2][list]
[*]For all pawn generation, the container scan is done once per map for each pawn or pawn group instead of dozens of times.
[*]With Humanoid Alien Races, starting apparel no longer copies and filters the full apparel list for every pawn: the race-filtered list is built once per race (same HAR rule, same result).
[*]Pawn graphics are built the first time a pawn is drawn, 2-5 ms each with HAR. Deferred pawns get them during generation; other pawns on the map are prepared in the background at about 1 ms per frame.
[*]Arriving groups start looking for their first job spread over half a second, and the "remove died thoughts" check on spawn only looks at pawns that remember one of them, instead of every pawn in the world.
[*]Raider steal checks compute each nearby item's market value once per check instead of once per raider (corpses included). Same idea as [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3781897112]Raider Approach Lag Fix[/url] by OldManYoung, extended to the stealing phase; the two work together.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Applies to enemy and friendly raids and beggar/refugee quests with at least 5 pawns. Smaller groups, trader caravans, quest raids, and raid types that create their own threats (for example mechanoid sieges) run immediately as in vanilla.
[*]With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3220299022]Dynamic Diplomacy - Continued[/url], the armies of a full-simulation conquest arena are generated the same way. The arena map itself is still generated in one go when the battle starts.
[*]Each generation step still costs one pawn's generation time (usually 15-30 ms, occasionally over 100 ms), so you may notice a light stutter while a large group is being prepared. While you move or zoom the camera, steps wait until it has been still for 0.4 s (at most 10 s per group).
[*]An autosave that comes due while pawns are being generated waits until they are done (at most a minute). A manual save in that window finishes them first, so nothing is lost.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[/list]

[h2]Test report[/h2]One late-game colony with a large mod list (Humanoid Alien Races, Miho, Dynamic Diplomacy; 170 active mods in total), about 1,100-1,500 world pawns. Numbers are real-time milliseconds from development-mode logs. Also active: Performance Optimizer, Performance - Slower Pawn Tick Rate, Performance Esmolas, Better GC.

[h3]Raids (2026-10-05, 2026-10-07)[/h3][list]
[*]Vanilla: friendly raids of 70 / 93 pawns froze for 2.9 / 2.6 s; Dynamic Diplomacy arenas of 234-258 pawns for 12.3 s, 31.6 s with several old arena maps loaded.
[*]Enemy raids of 10-144 pawns: arrival pause 19-140 ms; friendly raids of 23-96 pawns: 17-57 ms. 111 pawns were generated over 21.5 s.
[*]Generation steps: 25-27 ms on average in 138-165-pawn raids; worst single step 320 ms (a garbage collection).
[*]Dynamic Diplomacy arenas of 255-324 pawns: armies generated over 34-73 s; the battle start (map generation) still paused 1.7-7 s.
[*]Saving or loading while groups were pending: saved groups arrived intact, nothing carried over into a loaded game; a due autosave waited 19.1 s, then saved.
[*]Pawn graphics: a 140-pawn raid arriving on screen took a 737 ms frame without the warmup, 29-35 ms with it.
[*]Steal checks (82-pawn raid): 2.5-2.8 ms per check without the cache, 1.4-1.5 ms with it.
[/list]
[h3]Beggar and refugee quests (2026-10-09)[/h3][list]
[*]Vanilla: groups of 12-30 froze for 0.74-1.07 s when the quest appeared; accepting 43 refugees froze for 0.59 s.
[*]With this mod: groups of 12-52 generated over 6-24 s (steps 11-31 ms on average), quest appeared with a 0.2-0.35 s pause; accepting 51 refugees took 78 ms, all walked in over 2.6 s.
[/list]
[h3]Crowded maps (2026-10-09)[/h3][list]
[*]Dynamic Diplomacy arenas with 46-90 walking, waiting or downed pawns: their update went from 12-17 to 6.5-10 µs per pawn per tick, saving 0.4-0.6 ms of a 9-14 ms tick (about 4-5%). No visible change in movement or animation.
[/list]
